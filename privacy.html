Privacy Policy

Draft policy for the WasteConnect prototype.

Status: draft for review. This page describes the intended handling of information and must be completed and reviewed before launch.

1. Who operates WasteConnect

WasteConnect is being developed as a business-to-business marketplace for discovering and requesting industrial by-products and reusable materials. Before this policy is published for a live service, the operator's full legal name, registered business address, and a monitored privacy contact must be added and verified.

2. Information that may be collected

When live, the platform may collect information needed to create and manage business accounts, including work email, name and role of a contact person, business phone number, company name, business address, industry, website, and preferences to buy or sell materials. Company verification may involve business registration identifiers, GSTIN where applicable, and supporting documents.

Listings and purchase requests may include material descriptions, grade, quantity, indicative price, collection location, photos, communications between businesses, and records of requests or completed transactions. Only information necessary for the service should be requested.

3. How information may be used

Information may be used to create company profiles, manage account access, review business details, display material listings, route purchase requests, communicate service updates, handle complaints, detect misuse, maintain records, and improve the platform. Any additional use must be explained before the live service collects the relevant information.

4. Verification documents

Business documents and identifiers can contain confidential information. The live platform must use appropriate access controls, secure transmission and storage, a documented review process, and a retention schedule. Do not upload sensitive business documents to this prototype: file selections are not transmitted or stored.

5. Sharing and service providers

Information may need to be shared with the business counterparties involved in a request and with service providers that support hosting, communications, verification, customer support, or payments, if those services are introduced. Before launch, the operator must identify the actual providers, explain the information each receives, and document any other sharing required by law.

6. Retention and security

Information should be retained only for a defined business or legal purpose and deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable obligations. No website can promise absolute security. Before launch, WasteConnect must implement and test security controls, backups, access permissions, incident handling, and a specific retention and deletion process.

7. Your choices and requests

The live service should provide a working way for account holders to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their information where applicable, and to raise privacy concerns. Add and test the privacy contact and response procedure before publication. Identity checks may be needed to prevent unauthorised requests.

8. Cookies and analytics

This static prototype does not use analytics or set account cookies. If the live platform introduces analytics, advertising, or non-essential cookies, this policy and any consent controls must be updated to match the actual tools used.

9. Children and business accounts

WasteConnect is intended for business use and is not designed for children. A live operator must set account eligibility rules and take appropriate steps if it learns that an account has been created improperly.

10. Changes to this policy

The published policy should show its effective date and be updated when the actual service, data uses, service providers, or legal requirements change. Material changes should be communicated in an appropriate way.

11. Contact

Before launch, replace the launch checklist item with the verified legal operator name and a monitored privacy email and postal address. Do not publish this draft as a final policy until those details and the actual data practices have been confirmed.
