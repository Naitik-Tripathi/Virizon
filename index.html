How it works

A simple workflow for business-to-business exchange.

The first prototype focuses on discovery and purchase requests. Payments, logistics, and verification need secure backend services before real trades can take place.

STEP 01

Create a company profile

Enter business and contact details, choose whether your company wants to buy, sell, or do both, and submit the information for review.

STEP 02

Publish or discover materials

Suppliers can describe material type, quality, quantity, indicative price, and location. Buyers can filter listings against their requirements.

STEP 03

Send a purchase request

Interested businesses can request quantities and discuss terms. Both parties must confirm compliance, delivery, and payment arrangements.

Built for industrial teams

Useful details before the first conversation.

Virizon is being designed around business information that helps companies assess a potential material exchange.

Clear material descriptions

Record the material type, grade, condition, quantity, photos, and relevant handling information. Do not advertise a listing without checking its details.

Business identity and review

Company details and supporting records can help an operator review accounts. A real verification workflow must be implemented before using a verified badge.

Requests before commitments

A purchase request is an initial business conversation, not proof that payment, pickup, delivery, or a sale has been completed.
