Terms & Conditions

Draft terms for the WasteConnect prototype.

Status: draft for review. This page is not the final contract for a live marketplace.

1. About the service

WasteConnect is being developed as a business-to-business marketplace concept to help companies discover, list, and send requests about industrial by-products and reusable materials. The current website is a prototype. Registration, email verification, company verification, payments, messaging, and transactions are not connected to a live backend.

2. Eligibility and account information

A live account should be created only by a person authorised to act for the relevant business and who meets the service's eligibility requirements. You must provide accurate, current information, update it when it changes, and keep account credentials confidential. Do not impersonate another business or submit documents you are not authorised to provide.

3. Company review and verification

Submitting company details or documents does not automatically verify a business. A live verification process must define the checks performed, how status is communicated, how long review takes where a time estimate is provided, and how a business can correct or appeal a decision. Until that process is implemented, no verification status in the prototype should be treated as official.

4. Listings and material descriptions

Businesses that publish listings must have the right to offer the materials and must provide honest information about type, grade, condition, quantity, photographs, price, location, and any known handling requirements. A listing must not be misleading or offer materials the business is not authorised to sell or transfer.

5. Legal handling of materials

Each business is responsible for complying with applicable laws, permits, environmental requirements, transport rules, and safety obligations for the materials it offers or purchases. Materials that require special authorisation must only be handled by appropriately authorised parties. WasteConnect must define restricted material categories and escalation procedures before enabling live listings.

6. Purchase requests and transactions

A purchase request is an expression of interest, not a confirmed sale or proof of payment. Unless a separate written agreement says otherwise, the buyer and seller must independently agree on quantity, price, quality, inspection, packaging, collection, delivery, tax, payment timing, and any cancellation terms. The parties are responsible for accurately documenting their agreement.

7. Fees, payments, and delivery

The current prototype does not process payments or charge marketplace commissions. If fees, escrow, payment collection, logistics, or other paid services are introduced, the operator must publish the applicable fees and terms before users commit to a transaction. Do not imply that payments or delivery are protected or guaranteed unless that service is actually implemented.

8. Acceptable use

Users must not upload malicious files, attempt to access another account, disrupt the service, misrepresent business identity, misuse confidential information, or use the platform to arrange unlawful activity. Users must respect the rights of other parties and only upload information and photographs they are allowed to share.

9. Third-party services

The live platform may rely on external hosting, identity verification, communications, payment, or logistics providers. Their actual role, terms, and data handling must be disclosed where relevant. Links to third-party services do not mean WasteConnect endorses every product or service offered by those providers.

10. Availability and limitations

The prototype is provided for demonstration and may contain incomplete features. The live operator should accurately describe service availability and should not claim that a listing is available, a business is verified, a payment is protected, or a transaction has completed unless that status has been checked by the system.

11. Suspension and complaints

A production service should define when accounts or listings may be restricted, how users will be notified, how complaints are handled, and what records are kept. Provide a monitored business contact and a documented complaint process before enabling real transactions.

12. Changes to these terms

Published terms should show an effective date, identify the legal operator, explain how updates are communicated, and preserve any rights that cannot legally be excluded. Users should be informed of material changes before they take effect where required.

13. Governing law and operator details

Before launch, insert the correct legal entity name, registered address, governing law, dispute resolution process, and any required consumer or business notices after appropriate legal review. These details are intentionally not guessed in this draft.

14. Contact

Add a monitored support email and business address before publishing final terms. Do not use this draft as a substitute for legal advice tailored to the registered operator, target users, materials covered, and countries or states in which the service will operate.
